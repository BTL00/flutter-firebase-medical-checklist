## 1. Product name and identification

Product name, Basic UDI-DI (if assigned), platforms (iOS, Android, web), software functions covered by this statement.

## 2. Intended purpose (one sentence)

> *[Product] is intended to [action: record / monitor / calculate / support decisions about] [clinical parameter or condition] in [patient population] to [clinical goal], for use by [intended users] in [use environment].*

## 3. Medical indication(s)

Conditions, stages, risk groups. State the clinical problem the software addresses.

## 4. Intended patient population

Age range, sex, pregnancy status, comorbidities, inclusion and exclusion criteria.

## 5. Intended users

Patients, caregivers/proxies, healthcare professionals (specialty, minimum qualification), administrators. Required training.

## 6. Use environment

Home, outpatient clinic, hospital ward, ambulance, remote/unsupervised. Connectivity assumptions.

## 7. Operating principle

What the software does with the data, in plain language. Inputs (manual entry, connected devices, platform health data, EHR), processing (storage, display, calculation, algorithm, AI model), outputs (values, trends, alerts, recommendations) and to whom.

## 8. Claims

Every performance and clinical claim you will make anywhere (store, website, sales). Each claim needs evidence (see checklist file 12).

## 9. Explicit exclusions and limitations

What the software is **not** for (e.g. not for emergency use, not a replacement for clinical judgement, not for children under X, not validated for Y). Contraindications and warnings.

## 10. Supported devices and minimum requirements

Phone/tablet models or classes, OS versions, connected medical devices (with regulatory status), network and security requirements (MDR Annex I §17.4).
